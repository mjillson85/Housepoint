Housepoint privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026 · applies to the Housepoint website and iPhone app

The short version

Housepoint shows public property records for the house you point at. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It never asks for or shows owner names. We don't collect your data.

Location, camera and compass

Housepoint uses your location, camera and compass to work out which house you're pointing at. The camera picture and compass readings never leave your phone. In point mode (and when you tap “Near me” on the map), your phone's current position is the map location sent to the public record services below, so they can return the properties around you.

What gets sent to public data services

To show records, Housepoint asks public government and mapping services for the properties around a map location (the spot you're looking at or searched for): the Massachusetts, Connecticut, New Jersey and Florida parcel services, building outlines, aerial photos, FEMA flood zones and USGS elevation. Several of those parcel layers and the building outlines are hosted on ArcGIS Online (Esri's hosting service), where the app also looks up their current addresses. If you open “Thinking of buying?”, the house's map location is also sent to federal school data (NCES), the Census Bureau (and, if that lookup fails, the FCC's census-area lookup), the FBI's crime data service, Geoapify (for nearby places), volunteer-run OpenStreetMap Overpass servers as a backup for nearby places (overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee, overpass.kumi.systems), and the USGS National Map for the nearby map's background tiles. The monthly cost calculator runs entirely on your phone; nothing you type into it is sent anywhere. These requests contain map coordinates, and like any web request they come from your device's internet address. They don't include your name, account or contact details, because Housepoint doesn't have any. Each service has its own policies.

Owner names

Some public records include owner names. Housepoint only requests the fields it displays (address, year built, value, sale, size and similar), so owner names are never downloaded to your phone and never shown.

Saved houses

Houses you look up are kept in a History list on your phone so you can find them again, and houses you star are kept in a Saved list to compare. Your calculator settings (down payment, rate and loan length) are remembered the same way. All of it stays on your device. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, erases it.

Sharing

If you tap Share, Housepoint makes an image of the card on your phone and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Children

Housepoint isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Questions: open an issue at github.com/mjillson85/Housepoint.
