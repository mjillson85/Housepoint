Housepoint privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026 · applies to the Housepoint website and iPhone app

The short version

Housepoint shows public property records for the house you point at. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It never asks for or shows owner names. We don't collect your data.

Location, camera and compass

Housepoint uses your location, camera and compass to work out which house you're pointing at. The camera picture and compass readings never leave your phone. Your location is used on your phone to pick nearby houses.

What gets sent to public data services

To show records, Housepoint asks public government and mapping services for the properties around a map location (the spot you're looking at or searched for): the Massachusetts and Florida parcel services, building outlines, aerial photos, FEMA flood zones and USGS elevation. These requests contain map coordinates, and like any web request they come from your device's internet address. They don't include your name, account or contact details, because Housepoint doesn't have any. Each service has its own policies.

Owner names

Some public records include owner names. Housepoint only requests the fields it displays (address, year built, value, sale, size and similar), so owner names are never downloaded to your phone and never shown.

Saved houses

Houses you look up are saved in a list on your phone so you can find them again. That list stays on your device. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, erases it.

Sharing

If you tap Share, Housepoint makes an image of the card on your phone and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Children

Housepoint isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Questions: open an issue at github.com/mjillson85/Housepoint.
