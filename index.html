Hold your phone up at a house in Massachusetts or Florida and see when it was built, what it last sold for and what it’s valued at.

Uses your camera, location and compass on this device only. Property facts come from public assessor records (MassGIS in Massachusetts, the Dept. of Revenue in Florida). Owner names are never shown.
